Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy covers every account run through Social Studio, including @ur_dailydog on Instagram and its linked Facebook Page. Social Studio is run by the owner of @ur_dailydog.

What we collect

We only collect information from people who choose to submit to a contest or ask to be featured:

  • your dog's name
  • your Instagram handle
  • the photo or video you upload
  • the time you ticked the consent boxes

We also see the aggregate insights Instagram and Facebook give us about our own posts, such as reach and likes. We do not collect anything about other Instagram users through the Meta API.

Why we collect it

  • to review entries
  • to post features with credit to your handle
  • to run random draws, including the 90-day cooldown that stops a weekly winner from winning again too soon

Where it is stored

Entries and uploaded files are stored with Supabase, a hosted database and file storage provider.

Who sees it

Only us. We never sell your information. The only time it is shared is when we post the feature you agreed to.

How long we keep it

  • Entries that are not featured are deleted within 90 days.
  • Featured posts stay up until the owner asks for removal (see below).

Meta Graph API

We use the Meta Graph API only to publish to our own accounts and to read insights for our own accounts. We do not use it to collect data about other Instagram users.

Cookies

None. This site has no trackers, analytics or ads.

Children

Contest entrants must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Contact and removal

Send a DM to the account on Instagram, for example @ur_dailydog. We will remove your information and any feature post within 48 hours. See Data deletion for the steps.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
