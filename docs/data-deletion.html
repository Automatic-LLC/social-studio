Data deletion

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time.

How to ask

  1. Send a DM on Instagram to the account you entered, for example @ur_dailydog.
  2. Say you want your data deleted and include your Instagram handle.

What happens next

Within 48 hours we will:

  • delete your entries and the files you uploaded
  • remove any feature post of your submission
  • reply to your DM to confirm

Facebook and Instagram login

This site has no login, so we do not hold any Facebook Login user data. The only personal information we have is what you gave us in a contest entry. See the Privacy Policy for details.
