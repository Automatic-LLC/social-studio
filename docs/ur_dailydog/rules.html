Dog of the Week: official rules

Submission page coming soon.

Who can enter

  • Entrants must be 18 or older.
  • You must own the photo or video you submit.

Your submission

  • By entering, you give @ur_dailydog a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to post, edit and share your submission on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube, with credit to your handle.
  • Entries are screened. We can disqualify anything that is not a dog photo or video, or that shows children's faces, people who did not agree to appear, addresses, or anything violent or sexual.
  • DM @ur_dailydog to have your submission removed at any time.

Prize

No purchase necessary. The prize is a feature on the account. There is no cash or goods.

Weekly schedule

  • Entries open Monday and close Thursday night (US Central time).
  • Winners are drawn at random from eligible entries on Friday and posted Sunday evening, with the owner tagged.
  • Each week there is one winner and two honorable mentions.
  • Winners are notified by the tag on the winning post.

Repeat winners and Dog of the Month

  • A weekly winner cannot win again for 90 days, matched by Instagram handle. Honorable mentions have no cooldown.
  • Dog of the Month is drawn at random from that month's weekly winners.

Not affiliated with Meta

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, Facebook or Meta.
